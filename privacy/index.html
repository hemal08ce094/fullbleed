Privacy policy

Updated 1 November 2026

Fullbleed collects no data. Nothing about you, your phone or the pages you visit is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What is stored on your phone

Your settings and your per-site modes are stored on the device, in an App Group shared by the Fullbleed app and its Safari extension. This includes the list of site addresses you set a mode for, your default mode, and a few counters the app shows you, such as how many pages Fullbleed has widened and the last page it widened. This data never leaves the phone, except as described for Apple Watch below. Deleting the app removes it.

If you install the Apple Watch app, the iPhone sends your site list, your default mode and the names of recently widened sites to your own paired Apple Watch over Apple's watch connection, and to nothing else. The watch keeps them, along with the addresses of pages you open on the watch and a count of them, on the watch only. Pages you open on the watch load directly from those websites in a private session, as they would in any browser. Deleting the watch app removes its data.

What Fullbleed does not do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No accounts.
  • No network requests from the app or the extension.
  • No logging of page content. The extension reads a page's viewport tag and layout width to change the width, and that stays in the page.

Reports

"Report this site" prepares an email in your own Mail app. It contains the address of the site you chose to report, the mode, your iOS version and the app version. It is only sent if you press send. The developer receives it as an ordinary email to the support address.

Children

Fullbleed does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
